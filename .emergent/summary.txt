<product_requirements>
The application is a production and warehouse management system for a meat processing facility, designed to track products from raw material to packaged goods.

**Core Workflow:**
1.  **Butchery (Обробка):** Raw meat is processed into cuts.
2.  **Production (Виробництво):** Cuts are used in recipes to create bulk products (e.g., Бастурма).
3.  **Packaging (Фасування):** Bulk products are packaged into sellable SKUs.

**Key Feature Implementation:**
The primary focus of the work was a complete refactor of the Packaging module. The initial, simplistic model was replaced with a Packaging Session concept. In a single session, an operator can take a quantity of one bulk product and package it into multiple different SKUs, recording the output quantity for each, plus any waste or usable remnants.

**Critical Data Requirement:**
A major part of the project involved a deep database cleanup to establish a single source of truth for all product nomenclature. This was driven by the user's need for an accurate and non-redundant list of raw materials, semi-finished goods, bulk production outputs, and final packaged SKUs, ensuring all production and packaging recipes link to the correct items.
</product_requirements>
